# Project Brief

## What It Is

A Hebrew-first, SEO-driven directory of licensed drone service providers in Israel. Users search by service type and location; businesses get listed and gain leads.

## Problem

- No dedicated, trustworthy Hebrew directory for drone services in Israel
- Businesses have no single place to be found; customers have no easy way to find vetted providers
- The market is fragmented across Facebook groups, generic classifieds, and word-of-mouth

## Target Users

- **Demand side**: individuals and businesses searching for drone services (photography, surveying, inspections, agriculture, events)
- **Supply side**: licensed drone operators and companies wanting inbound leads

## Business Model (current assumptions)

- **Featured listings**: paid upgrade for higher placement and visual prominence
- **Sponsored placements**: paid spots at top of category/city pages
- **Future**: pay-per-lead (contact unlocking), once traffic validates demand

## MVP Limitations

- No user accounts or login
- No payments (featured/sponsored handled manually)
- No in-app messaging
- No reviews or ratings
- No mobile app (mobile-responsive web only)

## Language & Locale

- **Primary language**: Hebrew (RTL)
- **Internal code**: English (variable names, file names, API keys, schema fields)
- **URL slugs**: English (SEO-friendly, transliterated or translated)
- **Target locale**: Israel (IL), currency ILS where relevant

## Success Metrics (MVP)

- Indexed pages ranking for long-tail Hebrew drone queries
- Organic traffic to city + service combination pages
- Manual conversion of at least 3–5 featured listings within 3 months of launch
